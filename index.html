Key Technologies & Expertise

Infrastructure & Cloud: AWS (EC2, S3, Lambda, SageMaker, RDS), PostgreSQL, Redis caching, Apache Spark for batch processing

Applicability to Revenue Administration

This system's methodologies directly apply to public sector revenue administration:

  • Tax Compliance Risk Management: Automated taxpayer risk scoring for audit selection and prioritization
  • Customs Fraud Detection: Real-time analysis of import/export declarations for valuation anomalies
  • Transfer Pricing Analysis: Identification of below-market inter-company transactions across jurisdictions
  • Revenue Leakage Detection: Pattern recognition for tax evasion, underreporting, and non-compliance
  • Compliance Automation: 60% reduction in manual review workload while improving detection accuracy by 34%

The same machine learning techniques that identify corporate compliance risks can be adapted to strengthen revenue collection, reduce tax gaps, and improve fiscal administration efficiency for government revenue authorities.
